A little about me

I work at the intersection of computer vision and image generation. I'm especially interested in controllable generation and generative super-resolution with diffusion transformers and flow matching.

Before Dartmouth, I explored high-dimensional statistics with Prof. Ziping Zhao at the DSAI Lab, ShanghaiTech University. That work led to an ICML 2025 paper on tensor regression.

From statistical foundations to visual imagination.
